# App Illustration Prompts

Wide-format prompts for the personal applications in the app suite.

**Common visual direction:** clean, modern editorial vector illustration; wide panoramic composition at approximately 2.5:1–3:1; warm neutral/off-white background; several muted complementary colours; clean geometric shapes; refined consistent line art; flat vector forms; subtle layered depth; soft shadows; rounded forms; generous negative space; no text, letters, numbers, readable labels, logos, UI screenshots, photorealism, gradients, 3D rendering, black-and-white imagery or monochrome treatment.

## x. Reel — TV Series and Movie reviewer

Create a clean, modern editorial vector illustration representing Reel, a personal movie and TV series discovery collection.

The application helps users keep track of movies and TV series they want to watch, discover new entertainment, and find out which streaming platforms offer each title. It connects personal watchlists with external information sources, including IMDb, for film details, ratings, descriptions and imagery.

Create a cohesive composition centred around a cinematic film reel or a small stack of movie cases, representing a personal collection of entertainment. Surround this central element with carefully arranged objects that suggest discovering, collecting, organising and watching films and TV series.

Include a balanced selection of visual elements such as:

Movies and TV series: overlapping cinema-style frames, a film strip, a television screen, or a few elegant movie-poster shapes featuring abstract scenes such as a dramatic landscape, a city at night, a spaceship or a mysterious silhouette. Keep the scenes illustrative rather than photorealistic.
Discovery and recommendations: a magnifying glass, a small collection of curated film cards, a subtle star-shaped rating symbol, or a spotlight suggesting the discovery of something worth watching.
Watchlists and collections: a neatly organised stack of film cards, a bookmark, a small notebook, or a ticket tucked into a movie case.
Streaming availability: several small, distinct screens or media cards connected by subtle visual pathways, suggesting that the same title can be found across different streaming services. Represent the services with abstract coloured symbols rather than actual platform logos.
External film information: a small reference card or open information booklet suggesting film descriptions, cast information, ratings and links to detailed external sources. Use abstract marks rather than readable text.

The illustration should communicate personal curation, discovery, organisation and the enjoyment of choosing something great to watch. It should feel like an enthusiast's carefully maintained movie and TV collection rather than a cinema advertisement, streaming service homepage or commercial film poster.

Arrange the elements naturally around the central cinematic object, with a sense of depth and visual flow. Make the individual objects recognisable without overcrowding the composition. Balance the cinematic atmosphere with a warm, approachable feeling appropriate for a personal web application.

Use clean geometric shapes, refined consistent line art, flat vector forms, subtle layered depth, soft shadows, rounded forms and generous negative space. Maintain the sophisticated editorial illustration style of the other applications in the suite.

Use a warm neutral/off-white background with several muted complementary colours. Incorporate colours inspired by cinema and entertainment, such as muted burgundy, deep teal, dusty blue, ochre, terracotta and warm cream. Ensure that the objects have clearly distinguishable colours and that the overall image remains colourful, understated and contemporary—not black-and-white, monochromatic or overly bright and playful.

Composition: wide panoramic format, approximately 2.5:1–3:1 aspect ratio, designed for a web application landing-page banner. Distribute the objects naturally across the horizontal canvas, with generous breathing room and a clear visual centre. Keep the composition balanced and cohesive when displayed in a wide website header.

Avoid: text, letters, numbers, readable labels, real streaming platform logos, real movie or TV show titles, recognisable copyrighted characters, actual movie posters, brand logos, UI screenshots, dashboards, photorealism, gradients, 3D rendering, black-and-white imagery and monochrome treatment.
