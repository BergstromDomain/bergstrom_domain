**Contributions** are things you gave rather than received: talks, workshops, panels, articles, podcasts, meetups you organised, open-source work and mentoring. They stand on their own, outside your Engagements, because they often belong to neither your employer nor your client at the time.

## Adding One

Choose **Actions › New › Add A Contribution**, or **Add A Contribution** on the empty Contributions page.

- **Kind:** Talk, Workshop, Panel, Article, Podcast, Meetup Organised, Open Source or Mentoring.
- **Title:** what it was called, such as "Statistical Methods in Software Testing".
- **Description** (optional): what it covered.
- **Files** (optional): the poster, slides or a photo, as PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF, up to 10 MB each. Only you and admins can open them.
- **Event** (optional): the meetup, conference or publication, such as All Things Testing Brisbane. It's an Organisation: if it isn't listed, add it with **Add An Organisation** first.
- **Date:** a year on its own is fine; add the month and day if you know them.
- **Location**, **Presented As** and **Link** (optional): where it was, the title you were billed under, and a recording, the slides or the event page.
- **Skills** (optional): the Skills it shows, separated by commas, such as "Robot Framework, Knowledge Sharing". Professional Skills work here too.
- **During** (optional): the Engagement you made it during, for context. The Contribution still stands on its own, and stays if that Engagement is deleted.

## Your Contributions

**My Career › Contributions** lists them grouped by kind, newest first, with each one's event, date and Skills. Choose one to open its page, where you can **Edit** or **Delete** it.

Deleting is soft: **Actions › Deleted Contributions** lists them for 30 days, and **Restore** brings one back. After 30 days it's removed for good, with its files.
