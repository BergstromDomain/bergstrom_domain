Cookbook is a collection of recipes. Anyone can read the recipes they're allowed to see, signed-in users can react to and comment on them, and Content Creators can write their own.

## Who sees which recipe

Every recipe has a **Visibility** chosen by its chef:

- **Unrestricted**: visible to everyone, including visitors who aren't signed in.
- **Contacts**: visible to the chef's confirmed contacts.
- **Restricted**: visible only to the chef.

A recipe is a **Draft** until its chef publishes it. Drafts are visible only to their chef, whatever their visibility. Admins can see every recipe.

## Finding your way around

The left nav has the same shape as the other apps:

- **Views**
  - **Cookbook**: the Cookbook landing page.
  - **Recipes**: Browse Recipes By Cuisine, Browse Recipes By Food Type, Filter Recipes and Find Recipes By Ingredients. See *Finding Recipes*.
  - **My Recipes** (signed in): My Published Recipes and My Draft Recipes.
  - **Reference Data**: the Cuisines, Food Types and Languages that recipes are filed under.
- **Actions** (Content Creator and above)
  - **New**: Write A Recipe. See *Writing A Recipe*.
  - **Import & Export**: Download Recipes. See *Downloading Recipes*.
- **Documentation**: this User Guide.

You can choose which Cookbook page opens when you sign in. See *Cookbook Settings*.
