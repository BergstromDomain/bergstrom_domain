Signed in, open **Cookbook Settings** from the Settings section of the left nav.

- **Start Page**: which page opens when you sign in with Cookbook as your start app. Choose the Cookbook landing page, Browse Recipes By Cuisine or Food Type, Filter Recipes, Find Recipes By Ingredients, or the Cuisines, Food Types or Languages lists.
- **Default Classification**: the Visibility a new recipe starts with. You can still change it on each recipe.
- **Default Language**: the Language a new recipe starts with. English unless you choose another.

Choose **Save Cookbook Settings** to keep your changes.
