Content Creators and above can write recipes. Choose **Actions › New › Write A Recipe** in the left nav.

## The form

- **Title** (required): must be unique among your own recipes.
- **Description**: a short introduction, shown under the title.
- **Recipe Image**: a JPEG, PNG or WebP smaller than 5MB. Without one, the recipe shows its Cuisine's flag instead.
- **Cuisine** and **Food Type** (one required, up to two of each): the first is the main one; choose a second for recipes that sit in two places, like Swedish and Italian.
- **Language**: the language the recipe is written in. It starts as your default (see *Cookbook Settings*).
- **Serves or Makes**: fill in Serves (e.g. 4) or, when baking, Makes (e.g. 24). If both are filled in, Makes is shown.
- **Cook Time**: free text, e.g. "1 hour 30 min".
- **Visibility**: Unrestricted, Contacts or Restricted. See *Cookbook Overview*.

### Ingredients

Each ingredient has a **Quantity** and an **Ingredient** name, plus an optional **Measurement** (g, dl, tbsp) and **Comment** (softened, finely chopped). Use **Add Ingredient** for another row and the bin icon to remove one.

Longer recipes can be split into several lists, each with its own heading. A lasagna might have "Meat Sauce", "White Sauce" and "Assembling". Use **Add Ingredient List** to start another and **Remove List** to delete one.

### Method

The steps, written in the **Raw** (Markdown) or **Formatted** editor. You can switch between them at any time, and either way it's saved as Markdown.

## Draft and Publish

**Save** keeps the recipe as a **Draft**, visible only to you, with a reminder at the top of its page. When it's ready, choose **Publish** on the recipe's page. Publishing needs a Method and at least one ingredient. **Unpublish** turns it back into a draft.

Editing a published recipe keeps it published.

## Edit and Delete

Choose **Edit** on your recipe's page to change it. Admins can edit any recipe.

**Delete** removes the recipe from Cookbook. An admin can restore it, exactly as it was, for 30 days. After that it's deleted permanently along with its ingredients, reactions, comments and image.
