Signed in, open **Occasions Settings** from the Settings section of the left nav.

- **Start Page**: which page opens when you sign in with Occasions as your start app. Choose the Occasions landing page, Events By Day, Week or Month, People, or Event Types.
- **Default Classification**: the Visibility a new person or event starts with. It's Restricted until you change it, and you can still change it on each one.

Choose **Save Occasions Settings** to keep your changes.

## Default Visibility

**Settings › Preferences › Default Visibility** decides which Visibility checkboxes start ticked on the People and All Events pages. All three are ticked until you change it.
