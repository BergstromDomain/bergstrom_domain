Events are listed by the day and month they fall on, so each event shows up again every year. Every list only shows events you're allowed to see.

## Events By Day, Week and Month

- **Events By Day** opens on today. Use the arrows either side of the date to step back or forward a day.
- **Events By Week** shows Monday to Sunday of the current week, with arrows to the previous and next week.
- **Events By Month** opens on the current month. Choose another month from the row of month names.

Each row shows the event's image (or its Event Type's icon), its title, its date, its Event Type and the people in it. Choose the title to open the event, a person to open their page, or the Event Type icon to see every event of that type.

The date includes the year when one is known, for example "12 May 1998", and leaves it out otherwise.

## All Events

Choose **All** in the row of month names to list every event, ordered through the year from January. The month names narrow it to one month again.

Signed in, you also get:

- **Visibility** checkboxes for Unrestricted, Contacts and Restricted. Tick any combination. They start from your **Default Visibility** in *Preferences*.
- **Hide muted** and **Show all**. See *Muting* below.
- A mute button on each row.

## An event's page

Shows the event's image and description, its date, Event Type, Visibility and people. Its owner, or an admin, can **Edit Event** or **Delete Event** from here. Deleting an event can't be undone.

## Muting

Signed in, you can mute events you'd rather not see. There are three kinds:

- **Mute an event** from the All Events list.
- **Mute a person** from the People list. Their events are hidden, unless someone else in the event isn't muted.
- **Mute an Event Type** from the Event Types list, under Reference Data. Every event of that type is hidden.

Muted events are left out of Events By Day, Week and Month. On All Events, choose **Show all** to see them again, with the button to unmute them. Muting only affects what you see.
