Occasions keeps track of people and the events in their lives: birthdays, graduations, weddings, concerts, trips. Anyone can look through the people and events they're allowed to see, and Content Creators can add their own.

## People and events

An **event** happens on a day and month, with an optional year, and belongs to one **Event Type** such as Birthday or Wedding. Every event has at least one person, and can have several. A wedding, for example, can belong to both people getting married.

Events come round every year. Events By Day shows everything that falls on today's date, whichever year it first happened.

## Who sees what

Every person and event has a **Visibility** chosen by whoever added it:

- **Unrestricted**: visible to everyone, including visitors who aren't signed in.
- **Contacts**: visible to the owner's confirmed contacts.
- **Restricted**: visible only to the owner.

Admins can see everything.

### Contacts

To share Contacts-only people and events with someone, you both need to be confirmed contacts. Open **Settings › Contact Management › Contacts**, search for them by name and send a request. They confirm it from their own Contacts page. If they had already sent you a request, sending one back connects you straight away. Either of you can remove the contact later.

## Finding your way around

- **Views**
  - **Occasions**: the Occasions landing page.
  - **Events**: Events By Day, Events By Week and Events By Month. See *Viewing Events*.
  - **People**: the People list and Filter People and Events. See *People* and *Filtering People And Events*.
  - **Reference Data**: the Event Types and Social Media Platforms.
- **Actions** (Content Creator and above)
  - **New**: Create Event and Create Person. See *Creating Events And People*.
  - **Import & Export**: Import People And Events and Download People And Events. See *Importing And Downloading*.
- **Documentation**: this User Guide.

You can choose which Occasions page opens when you sign in. See *Occasions Settings*.
