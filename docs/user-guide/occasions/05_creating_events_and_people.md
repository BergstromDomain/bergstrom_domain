Content Creators and above can add people and events, from **Actions › New** in the left nav. An event needs at least one person, so add the people first.

## Create Person

- **First Name** (required), **Middle Name** and **Last Name**. The full name must be unique across Occasions, whoever added the other person.
- **Person image**: a JPEG, PNG or WebP smaller than 5MB.
- **Description**.
- **Visibility**: Unrestricted, Contacts or Restricted. It starts as your **Default Classification** (see *Occasions Settings*).

### Social media

Social media accounts are added when editing a person, not when creating one. Choose **Edit** on their page, pick a **Platform** and type their **username**. A person can have one account on each platform.

## Create Event

- **Event Type** (required): Birthday, Wedding and so on.
- **People** (at least one): hold Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) to choose more than one.
- **Title** (required): must be unique across Occasions, whoever added the other event. Capitals don't make a title different.
- **Description**.
- **Day** and **Month** (required) and **Year**. Leave the year empty if you don't know it. The date must exist, so 31 April is rejected.
- **Classification**: Unrestricted, Contacts or Restricted. It starts as your **Default Classification**.
- **Event image**: a JPEG, PNG or WebP smaller than 5MB. Without one, the event shows its Event Type's icon.

## Edit and Delete

The owner of a person or event can edit or delete it from its page. Admins can edit and delete anyone's.

Deleting can't be undone.

- **Deleting an event** removes it for good.
- **Deleting a person** also takes them out of all their events. Any event left with nobody in it is deleted too.
