Content Creators and above can write blog posts. Choose **Actions › New › Create A Blog Post** in the left nav.

## The form

- **Title** (required): must be unique among your own posts.
- **Blog Image**: a JPEG, PNG or WebP smaller than 5MB. Without one, the post shows its Category's icon.
- **Category** (required).
- **Subject** and **Topic**: free text that files the post more precisely. A Topic needs a Subject.
- **Visibility**: Unrestricted, Contacts or Restricted. It starts as your **Default Classification** (see *Chronicle Settings*).
- **Add Author**: choose co-authors from your confirmed contacts, moving them from **Available** to **Co-Authors** with the arrow buttons. Type in the filter box to find someone quickly.
- **Body**: the post itself, written in the **Raw** (Markdown) or **Formatted** editor. You can switch between them at any time, and either way it's saved as Markdown.

## Draft and Publish

**Save Blog Post** keeps the post as a **Draft**, visible only to its authors, with a reminder at the top of its page. When it's ready, choose **Publish** on the post's page. Publishing needs a Body. **Unpublish** turns it back into a draft.

**Editing a published post turns it back into a draft.** Saving with **Update Blog Post** unpublishes it, so choose **Publish** again when you've finished your changes.

## Co-authors

Co-authors see the post while it's a draft, and are listed on it as authors. Co-authors who are Content Creators can also edit, publish and delete it.

## Edit and Delete

Choose **Edit** on the post's page to change it. Admins can edit any post.

**Delete** removes the post from Chronicle. An admin can restore it for 30 days. After that it's deleted permanently.
