Signed in, open **Chronicle Settings** from the Settings section of the left nav.

- **Start Page**: which page opens when you sign in with Chronicle as your start app. Choose the Chronicle landing page, Browse Blog Posts, Filter Blog Posts, My Published Posts, My Unpublished Posts (your drafts) or Blog Categories.
- **Default Classification**: the Visibility a new post starts with. It's Restricted until you change it, and you can still change it on each post.

Choose **Save Chronicle Settings** to keep your changes.
