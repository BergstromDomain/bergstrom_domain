Admins look after the Blog Categories, can restore deleted posts, and can download a full export.

## Blog Categories

Everyone can view the Categories under **Reference Data** in the Chronicle left nav. Admins add one from **Actions › New › Create A Blog Category**, and edit or delete them from each one's page.

A Category has a **Name**, a **Description** and an **Icon** name from [lucide.dev/icons](https://lucide.dev/icons/), for example `cpu` or `plane`. The icon stands in for a post that has no image.

- Names must be unique, ignoring capitals.
- Each icon can only be used by one Category.
- A Category **can't be deleted while any post uses it**, including deleted posts that can still be restored. Move those posts first.

## Deleted Posts

**Actions › New › Deleted Posts** lists posts that have been deleted in the last 30 days, with their authors, when they were deleted and how many days each has left before it's removed for good. **Restore** puts a post back exactly as it was, published or draft. After 30 days, deleted posts are purged automatically.

## Full Export

On **Download Blog Posts**, admins also get **Download Full Export**. See *Importing And Downloading Blog Posts*.
