Chronicle is a blog. Anyone can read the posts they're allowed to see, signed-in users can react to and comment on them, and Content Creators can write their own, alone or with co-authors.

## Who sees which post

Every post has a **Visibility** chosen by its author:

- **Unrestricted**: visible to everyone, including visitors who aren't signed in.
- **Contacts**: visible to the author's confirmed contacts.
- **Restricted**: visible only to the author.

A post is a **Draft** until it's published. Drafts are visible only to their authors, whatever their visibility. Admins can see every post.

Contacts are managed under **Settings › Contact Management › Contacts**: search for someone by name, send a request, and they confirm it from their own Contacts page.

## How posts are filed

Every post has a **Category**, such as Technology or Travel, and can be narrowed further with a free-text **Subject** and, below that, a **Topic**. For example: Technology › Ruby On Rails › Testing. Browse Blog Posts follows the same three levels.

## Finding your way around

- **Views**
  - **Chronicle**: the Chronicle landing page.
  - **Blog Posts**: Browse Blog Posts and Filter Blog Posts. See *Reading Blog Posts*.
  - **My Blog Posts** (signed in): My Published Posts and My Draft Posts.
  - **Reference Data**: the Blog Categories.
- **Actions** (Content Creator and above)
  - **New**: Create A Blog Post. See *Writing A Blog Post*.
  - **Import & Export**: Import Blog Posts and Download Blog Posts. See *Importing And Downloading Blog Posts*.
- **Documentation**: this User Guide.

You can choose which Chronicle page opens when you sign in. See *Chronicle Settings*.
